Our story

Named after Mel's niece.

Melony Coons opened Mairanda's Cutting Edge in 2012 and gave it her niece's name. It's been a family salon from the start, with cuts for grown-ups and kids in the same chairs.

Mel now works alongside Justine and Linda, in the same spot on Old Post Road.
